Location Data (Precise and Background Location)

While using Our Application, in order to provide core location-monitoring features of Our Service, We collect information regarding Your location with Your prior permission:

  • Precise Location (GPS): We collect real-time geographic coordinates from Your Device.
  • Background Location: The Application collects location data even when the app is closed, minimized, or not actively in use on the screen. This is required to ensure continuous location tracking, route history logging, and geofencing alerts.

We use this Location Data strictly to provide and improve the Service (such as displaying real-time device positions on the map and sending location-based notifications to authorized account administrators). We do not sell Your Location Data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

You can enable or disable access to this information at any time through Your Device settings; however, disabling Location Data will prevent the core monitoring features of the Application from functioning properly.
